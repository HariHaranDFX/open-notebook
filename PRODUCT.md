## Platform

web

## Users

Open Notebook serves knowledge workers and research teams in self-hosted, single-tenant-per-client commercial deployments. Members collect evidence, organize it in notebooks, develop notes and ideas, ask questions, and create grounded syntheses. Administrators configure AI providers, models, credentials, processing defaults, system settings, and—when WP2b is integrated—local groups. Authentication supports password and Microsoft Entra deployments. In the commercial fork, authenticated work is private to its owner by default; the WP2b sharing branch adds explicit user/group access rather than cross-client sharing.

## Product Purpose

Open Notebook is a privacy-focused AI research workspace. Its central experience is: collect sources, organize them in notebooks, ask and synthesize across the selected material, and verify the provenance of the result. Success means users can move from evidence to understanding and creative exploration without losing the connection between source truth, personal thinking, and AI-assisted synthesis.

## Positioning

Open Notebook is a self-hostable, provider-agnostic alternative to single-provider research assistants. It is notebook-centric rather than a general-purpose chat thread: collected evidence, user-authored notes, AI synthesis, and provenance remain connected while the customer retains control of data, deployment, providers, and branding.

## Operating Context

The product supports sustained desktop research with an intentional mobile experience. Users work with sources, notebooks, notes, source-derived insights, scoped chat, cross-knowledge Ask and Search, podcasts, and reusable transformations. Source ingestion, embeddings, insight generation, podcast generation, and maintenance rebuilds can be asynchronous and may be queued, processing, completed, partially successful, or failed. The background worker is therefore part of the functioning product, not an optional implementation detail. Daily research work is primary; configuration and administration support it without becoming the product's center.

## Core Product Model

- A **source** is evidence ingested from one or more URLs, uploaded files, or pasted text. A source can exist independently and can be associated with multiple notebooks. Removing it from a notebook is different from deleting it globally.
- A **notebook** is the primary research workspace. It brings selected sources, notebook-owned notes, and one or more chat sessions into a coherent context. Notebooks can be archived without being deleted.
- A **note** is notebook-owned thinking. Notes may be human-authored or saved from AI output; that distinction must remain visible.
- A **source insight** is generated by applying a reusable transformation to a source. It remains linked back to that source.
- A **transformation** is a named, reusable prompt workflow with an optional model choice. It can be tested in a playground and applied during source ingestion or later insight generation.
- A **chat session** is a persistent conversation scoped to one source or one notebook. Sessions can be created, selected, renamed, and deleted, and can carry a model override.
- A **podcast episode** is an asynchronous audio artifact generated from deliberately selected notebook context using reusable episode and speaker profiles.
- A **credential/model configuration** connects customer-controlled AI providers to language, embedding, speech, and related product functions. Secrets require encryption at rest.

## End-to-End Product Flows

1. **Collect and process evidence.** Add up to a batch of URLs or files, or paste direct text; optionally associate the new sources with notebooks, apply transformations, and choose whether to create embeddings. The UI reports per-item progress, partial success, failure, retry, and refresh states.
2. **Organize without duplicating truth.** Create, revisit, archive, and search notebooks; connect existing sources to one or more notebooks; write or save notes; and distinguish removing an association from deleting the underlying source.
3. **Compose the evidence context.** Before notebook synthesis, users explicitly choose each source as excluded, insights-only, or full-text and each note as excluded or included. Bulk controls and context-size indicators make the active evidence boundary visible.
4. **Ask and synthesize at the right scope.** Users can chat with a single source, chat within a notebook's selected context, or use knowledge-wide Ask. AI responses preserve clickable references to sources, notes, and source insights; useful responses can be copied or saved back as notes.
5. **Find known and related material.** Knowledge-wide Search can query sources, notes, and source insights using text or embedding-based vector search. Results expose type and relevance and reopen the underlying material; vector search clearly depends on embeddings.
6. **Turn research into reusable outputs.** Apply transformations to produce source-linked insights, or generate podcast episodes from selected context. Podcast workflows preserve generation status, retry and failure details, playback, outline/transcript information, and the episode/speaker configuration used.
7. **Configure and maintain the system.** Administrators configure processing engines, embedding defaults, uploaded-file retention, encrypted provider credentials, discovered models, default models, and model tests. Advanced maintenance exposes version/system information and scoped embedding rebuild progress.
8. **Authenticate and share deliberately.** Password and Microsoft Entra sign-in lead into the same product. The current WP3 base enforces owner-scoped content. The separate WP2b branch adds notebook/source sharing with viewer and editor roles, owner/admin ACL management, and admin-managed local groups; WP3 must absorb those capabilities without flattening permission differences.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve every capability and state in the verified surface map, all backend contracts, data relationships, authentication, ownership, and permission behavior during WP3. Integrate the completed WP2b sharing branch before redesign implementation is considered complete.
- Navigation, route hierarchy, containers, information architecture, and responsive behavior may change when the result materially improves the central research workflow.
- URL-addressable content, return-path behavior, keyboard access, and recoverable in-progress work must survive any navigation redesign even if their exact implementation changes.
- Provenance is functional: context scope, human-versus-AI authorship, resource type, processing state, permissions, and clickable AI references must not be reduced to decoration or hidden behind guesswork.
- Destructive actions must keep scope explicit. In particular, deleting a source globally, removing it from a notebook, deleting a notebook with exclusively associated sources, revoking access, and deleting a generated artifact are different actions.
- Provider choice and portability are core product constraints; provider-exclusive behavior requires an explicit product decision.
- The REST API remains a first-class interface; the UI is a client, not the only access path.
- Self-hosting and customer control must not depend on mandatory cloud telemetry or a specific AI provider.
- White-label identity is configuration-driven and must preserve accessibility and semantic status behavior.
- Light and dark modes, keyboard operation, reduced motion, WCAG AA contrast, and production-safe error disclosure are required.
- User-facing copy must use the existing localization system and remain synchronized across all supported locales.
- The commercial fork must not add GPL or AGPL dependencies.

## Brand Commitments

The current product name is Open Notebook; the eventual default commercial name remains open. Customer deployments may supply their own product name, logo, and constrained brand accent through configuration. The product should communicate precision, trust, calm, momentum, and curiosity. Controls use plain, action-named language. Errors state the problem and a safe recovery path without exposing infrastructure details. Empty states guide the next useful action.

## Product Principles

1. **Keep evidence connected.** Source truth, user thinking, and AI synthesis must remain distinguishable and traceable.
2. **Make complexity legible.** Precision and trust come from clear hierarchy, scope, ownership, state, and provenance.
3. **Protect research momentum.** Frequent work should remain close, responsive, recoverable, and free of unnecessary interruption.
4. **Encourage grounded curiosity.** Help users follow connections and develop ideas without making unsupported output appear authoritative.
5. **Prefer control and portability.** Preserve privacy, self-hosting, provider choice, standards-based extension, and customer ownership.

## Product Boundaries

- Open Notebook is a research assistant, not a general-purpose chatbot.
- It supports concise research notes but is not intended to replace a full document editor.
- It processes source files but is not a general-purpose file-storage service.
- It complements a user's wider workflow through its API and integrations rather than attempting to replace every tool.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product must meet WCAG AA for supported workflows, including visible keyboard focus, logical focus order, screen-reader semantics, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, reduced-motion behavior, accessible asynchronous feedback, and layouts that tolerate translated text and text scaling.

## Open Decisions

- The default commercial product name beyond Open Notebook.
- The exact limits of customer font and brand-color customization; these must be constrained by accessibility and system coherence.
